> **In short** — open the link in the invitation email, from the email. It is
> good for seven days and works once. If you already have an account, sign in
> with the address the invitation was sent to; if you do not, you create one on
> the same screen.

You do not need an account to read this page, and you do not need one to start.
Everything below assumes you have been sent an invitation and nothing else.

## Open the link from the email

Click it in the email rather than copying it out. An invitation link carries
its code after a `#`, and an address copied without everything after that
symbol is not a valid invitation — which is the single commonest reason one
does not work.

The screen that opens names the agency you have been invited to, the access the
invitation grants, who invited you, and the address it was sent to. Check that
address against your own before going further.

## Join when you already have an account

If you are signed in, the screen offers to join and shows which account you are
signed in as. If that is not the address the invitation went to, use **Sign in
as somebody else** first — an invitation can only be used once, and joining
with the wrong account uses it up.

If you are signed out, sign in on that screen and you are returned straight to
the invitation.

Joining changes nothing else about your account. Anything you already have
stays exactly as it is, including membership of any other agency.

## Create an account and join

If you have no account, the same screen creates one. It asks for your name, an
email address — use the one the invitation was sent to — and a password of at
least eight characters.

Then one more step, and it catches people out:

1. You are sent a confirmation link. Open it to activate the account.
2. Go back to the **invitation** email and open its link again.

The invitation is still waiting. The invitation email is the durable copy of
your invitation, which is why the way back in is to open it rather than to
retrace the sign-up.

## Know what you can do once you are in

Your invitation granted a specific level of access, and the screen named it
before you joined. What that reaches is listed on
[Who can do what](/help/who-can-do-what), and every page in this help centre
labels its tasks with what they need.

[How it all fits together](/help/how-pevvy-is-organised) is the five-minute
tour: agencies, the sidebar, and where each kind of record lives.

## If it goes wrong

**The email has not arrived.** Check the spam folder first. After that, ask
whoever invited you — the agency's Team page shows what is known about the
delivery, and if nothing could be sent they are handed a one-time link to pass
on by hand.

**The link says the invitation is not valid.** It may have been withdrawn,
already used, or replaced by a newer one. Sending a fresh invitation stops the
previous link working, so use the most recent email. Nothing about your account
has changed.

**It says the link is incomplete.** Everything after the `#` is missing. Open
it from the email instead of pasting it.

**It expired.** Invitations last seven days. Ask for another.

**It says it has already been used.** Some account is already in the agency
with it. If that was you under a different address, ask for a new invitation to
the address you actually use.

**I joined with the wrong account.** Ask for a fresh invitation to the right
address. You can leave the agency from the Team page under the account that
joined by mistake.

**Whoever invited me says they cannot send another yet.** There is a short
pause between sends, and an hourly ceiling for the whole agency. Both pass.

**I created an account but I am asked to set up an agency.** That screen
creates a *new* agency of your own, which is not what you want. Go back to the
invitation email and open its link.

**I cannot sign in at all.** See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting),
which covers password resets and the traps in them.
