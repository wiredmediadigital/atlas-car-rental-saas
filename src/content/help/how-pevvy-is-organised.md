> **In short** — everything belongs to an **agency**. Your account can belong
> to more than one, you hold one role in each of them, and nothing you record
> in one is visible from another. The sidebar is the whole product, grouped the
> way the work is grouped.

## Understand what an agency is

An agency is the unit everything hangs off: its vehicles, its customers, its
contracts, its costs, its team and its settings. Two agencies on the same
deployment share nothing.

Your account is separate from your membership of an agency. Leaving an agency
ends your access to it and changes nothing about your account or about any
other agency you belong to.

If you belong to more than one, the switcher at the top of the sidebar moves
between them. It is worth checking which agency you are in before concluding
that a record has gone missing.

## Find your way around the sidebar

The sidebar is the product. It is grouped as the work is grouped:

| Group | What is in it |
|---|---|
| — | **Overview**: the dashboard. |
| Operations | **Rentals**, **Calendar**, **Customers** — the day-to-day. |
| Fleet | **Vehicles**, **GPS tracking** — the cars. |
| Finance | **Expenses**, **Financing**, **Reports** — the money. |
| Agency | **Team**, **Billing**, **Settings** — the business itself. |

Two things are not in that list. **Notifications** is reached from the bell
beside the agency's name, because it is an inbox rather than a section. **Help
centre** sits at the foot, with the account menu.

The sidebar lists only the sections your access could use, so a colleague's may
be shorter or longer than yours. Everything you can see there, you can at least
read.

## Know what your role decides

Everyone in an agency holds exactly one of four roles, and each can do
everything the one below it can. A role is held in that agency alone.

[Who can do what](/help/who-can-do-what) lists every action in the product and
the role it needs. Every page in this help centre labels its tasks the same
way, so you can tell before you start whether something is yours to do.

Inside the product a control you cannot use is usually simply absent rather
than greyed out, which is why that reference page is worth a bookmark: it tells
you what exists and who can reach it, so a missing button is a question with an
answer rather than a mystery.

## Know where each record lives

| What you have | Where it lives | What it attaches to |
|---|---|---|
| A car | Vehicles | Its own documents, photographs and compliance dates |
| A renter or a company | Customers | Their identification documents |
| A hire | Rentals | One vehicle, one renter, its drivers, charges and payments |
| Money the agency spent | Expenses | The agency, or one vehicle, or one contract |
| A loan or a lease | Financing | One vehicle |
| A tracker | GPS tracking | One vehicle at a time |

Calendar and Reports hold nothing of their own. Both are views over the records
above, which is why a change made in one of them shows up everywhere
immediately.

## Learn the four rules that apply everywhere

These hold across every module, and knowing them saves a lot of puzzlement:

- **Each record carries one currency, and nothing is converted.** There is no
  exchange rate anywhere in {{productName}}. A figure covering two currencies
  is never shown as one number; you get a per-currency breakdown or a count.
- **Money is never deleted.** A mistaken payment or cost is *voided*, with a
  reason, and stays visible struck through. That is the correction path.
- **Dates are the agency's dates.** Collection times, "today", month boundaries
  and report periods all use the agency's own time zone, which is set in
  Settings.
- **The view you build goes in the address bar.** Filters, sorts and date
  ranges live in the URL on almost every list, so a view you have set up can be
  bookmarked or sent to a colleague and arrives the same.

## Find the help for the page you are on

Most module screens carry a small help link beside their title, which opens
this centre at the page for that module. The sidebar here lists everything, and
the search at `/help` covers all of it.

You do not need to be signed in to read any of it.

## If it goes wrong

**A record I created is nowhere.** Check which agency you are in. Records
belong to one agency and are not visible from another.

**My sidebar is shorter than a colleague's.** Entries you could not use are not
shown. [Who can do what](/help/who-can-do-what) says which role reaches which
section.

**A section is there but half the buttons are not.** Opening a module and
acting inside it are different permissions, and most modules span several.

**Everything is read-only.** That is the subscription, not your role. See
[Billing](/help/billing).
