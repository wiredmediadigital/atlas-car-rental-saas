> **In short** — currency, time zone and language are asked for when the agency
> is created, and the currency is the one worth getting right first: existing
> records keep the currency they were written in, and nothing is ever
> converted. Everything else can be filled in as you go.

If you were invited to an agency somebody else runs, this page is not yours —
see [Accept your invitation](/help/accept-your-invitation). Creating an agency
starts a new one of your own.

## Create the agency

Signing up asks for your name, your email, a password of at least eight
characters, and four things about the business: its name, its country, its
currency and its time zone.

You are sent a confirmation link; the agency is created and waiting, and
nothing else is needed until you open it.

Those four are all editable afterwards, in Settings. Two of them have
consequences that are worth a moment now:

- **Currency** applies to records written from then on. Change it later and the
  agency simply has two currencies in its books, because nothing restates
  history and nothing converts.
- **Time zone** is the agency's own idea of today. Collection and return times,
  the day the Calendar opens on, and every report's month boundaries come from
  it.

## Fill in the details that print on paperwork
<!-- permission: organization.update -->

The general section of Settings holds the trading name, the registered legal
name where it differs, the tax identifier, an email, a phone number, a website
and the business address.

![The general section of Settings](/help/settings-01-general.jpg)

These appear at the head of the contracts and receipts the agency issues, so a
blank line here is a blank line on the document a customer takes away.

## Add the logo
<!-- permission: organization.update -->

A PNG, JPEG or WebP of up to 2 MB. It is shown in the sidebar, on contracts and
on receipts.

## Write the contract wording before the first hire
<!-- permission: organization.update -->

This is the step most worth doing before a customer signs anything. The
contract-terms section holds the tax rate and what the tax is called, five
policies — fuel, mileage, late return, damage and deposit — the full terms and
conditions, and a one-line footer.

Each policy prints as its own clause, in that order, and an empty one is left
out rather than printed blank.

{{productName}} supplies no legal text. There is no template and no default
wording: the terms are the agency's own, written by it or by its lawyer.

Contracts already issued never change. New wording reaches the next contract,
not the last one.

## Work through the checklist on the dashboard
<!-- permission: organization.view -->

Until the agency has at least one vehicle and at least one contract, the
dashboard carries a six-step checklist: create the agency, add the business
details, upload the logo, add the first vehicle, record the first customer,
create the first contract.

Every tick is read from the agency's own records. A step is complete because
the data is there, never because a screen assumed it. The checklist retires
itself when the agency starts trading.

## Invite the people who will use it
<!-- permission: team.invite -->

An invitation is an email address and a level of access. You can only grant
access at or below your own, and the agency cannot be handed over this way —
ownership moves by transfer rather than by invitation.

[Team](/help/team) covers inviting, changing access and handing the agency on.

## If it goes wrong

**I changed the currency and the old figures look wrong.** They are not.
Records keep the currency they were written in and nothing is converted.
Reports will show one currency at a time and say which.

**The contract came out without my terms.** It was issued before the terms were
saved. Reissue it and the new version carries them.

**Times are an hour out.** Check the time zone in Settings before anything
else.

**Everything in Settings is greyed out.** Everyone in the agency may read the
settings; changing them takes more access, and a banner at the top of the page
says so. Knowing the agency's currency and time zone matters to whoever is
writing contracts, even if they cannot change them.

**I cannot find where to set the expiry-warning window or the tracking
freshness windows.** They are agency settings, but there is no screen to change
them in this build. They start at thirty days for a renewal warning, and ten
minutes and two hours for tracking.

**I never got the confirmation email.** Check the spam folder. The
confirm-email screen can send another.
