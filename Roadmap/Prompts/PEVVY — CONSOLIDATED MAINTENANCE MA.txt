## 5. Expected maintenance and vehicle lifecycle

### 5.1 No maintenance restriction

When a vehicle has no applicable maintenance restriction, no active rental custody and no other operational restriction:

- Display Available.
- Allow rental booking and checkout according to existing availability rules.

### 5.2 Future scheduled maintenance

When maintenance is scheduled for a future date:

- Immediately display upcoming maintenance information.
- Display the maintenance reference.
- Display scheduled start and expected completion.
- Preserve current availability where the vehicle is otherwise eligible.
- Block rental periods overlapping the scheduled maintenance period.
- Allow non-overlapping rental periods.

Do not immediately take a vehicle out of service solely because maintenance has been scheduled for a future date.

Example:

Maintenance is scheduled for December 1 from 7:22 PM to 9:22 PM.

A rental ending before maintenance begins should be allowed.

A rental overlapping the maintenance period should be blocked.

A rental beginning after expected maintenance completion may be allowed, subject to other restrictions and checkout revalidation.

### 5.3 Scheduled maintenance period reached

When the scheduled maintenance period begins:

- Display an appropriate maintenance-due or scheduled-maintenance restriction.
- Enforce the applicable reservation and checkout restrictions.
- Do not automatically mark maintenance as physically started.
- Preserve the distinction between scheduled and actual maintenance.

If scheduled maintenance is missed or not started, clearly display its scheduling state and require the operator to resolve or reschedule it.

Do not misrepresent missed scheduled work as completed or physically in progress.

### 5.4 Maintenance started

When an operator explicitly starts maintenance:

- Immediately display In maintenance as the effective vehicle status.
- Immediately enforce the actual maintenance restriction.
- Remove misleading Available and Bookable now indicators.
- Remove misleading ready-to-hire descriptions.
- Record the actual maintenance start timestamp.
- Display the active maintenance reference.
- Display expected completion information.
- Provide a link to Maintenance Details.
- Block rental checkout.

This must apply even when the original scheduled maintenance date is in the future.

Do not delay an explicitly started maintenance restriction until the original scheduled start date.

### 5.5 Maintenance overdue

If expected completion passes while maintenance remains in progress:

- Retain the maintenance restriction.
- Display In maintenance with an overdue indicator.
- Continue blocking checkout.
- Do not automatically return the vehicle to service.

### 5.6 Maintenance completed

When maintenance is completed:

- Record actual completion.
- Release the restriction associated with that maintenance record.
- Recalculate effective vehicle status.
- Restore normal availability only if all applicable restrictions have been resolved.

Do not blindly set the underlying vehicle status to Available.

Preserve:

- Other active maintenance records.
- Independent off-the-road restrictions.
- Legacy restrictions not explicitly resolved.
- Actual rental custody.
- Other operational restrictions.

### 5.7 Maintenance cancelled

When maintenance is cancelled:

- Record cancellation reason and audit information.
- Release only the applicable maintenance restriction.
- Preserve maintenance history.
- Recalculate effective vehicle status.

Cancelling maintenance must not automatically declare an unsafe vehicle roadworthy.

If a safety-related restriction remains, the vehicle must remain unavailable.
